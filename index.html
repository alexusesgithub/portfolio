Enterprise Access Governance Platform

Reconciling identity, account, application & entitlement data to answer: who has access, and should they?

Enterprises accumulate access across dozens of systems until nobody can say with confidence who can reach what. I designed and built a governance platform that ingests identity, account, application and entitlement data into one model, runs a reconciliation engine to surface risky access, and supports the full User Access Review workflow from exception to remediation.

Ansible Failure Whisperer

LLM-powered diagnostics that turn cryptic AWX failure logs into plain-English root causes.

Ansible/AWX failures bury the actual cause in walls of log output that take engineers real time to parse. I built a diagnostics tool that feeds failure output through a local LLM (Qwen 3.5B) and returns a clear explanation of what broke and why — with inference running on-prem, not in the cloud.

Decisio

A RAG-powered decision-intelligence platform that grounds LLM recommendations in real business documents.

Business teams have data and documents scattered everywhere, but turning that into an actual recommendation is manual and slow. Decisio retrieves the relevant context — business documents, competitor data — and grounds an LLM's reasoning in it, producing decision support that's specific to the business, not generic advice.
